Contact us

We thrive on attention

To join us

You should find all the information you need on our Join page, but if you don't, email Tony the Boss, tony@pigdyke.co.uk

To book us

Please contact Julian the Secretary to discuss your requirements:
email: secretary@pigdyke.co.uk
phone/text: 07471 076225
We have performed, given workshops and lectures for all age groups in the UK and abroad at small events and major festivals. We are happy to do any combination of performing, teaching, talking, and demonstrating. For safety reasons, we need a hard, reasonably flat level surface to dance on: we don't dance on grass or gravel. We may charge a small fee to cover our expenses.

Photos and Video

Send us photos or videos to photos@pigdyke.co.uk, we love 'em. See our gallery page for details.

Everything else
